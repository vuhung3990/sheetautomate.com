  1. Click Copy Headers and paste into cell A1 of a blank Google Sheet.
  2. Open Extensions > Apps Script from the Sheets menu bar.
  3. Replace Code.gs with Copy Script, then press Save (Ctrl+S).
  4. Reload your Sheet and click the new SheetAutomate menu (nothing runs until you click).
  5. First time only: if Google shows “App isn’t verified”, click Advanced > Go to project to authorize only yourself.
